Where Students Go

Four Core Modules Across Modern China

Each city module offers a distinct lens on China. Core modules are typically 5 days and can be combined into longer programmes.

Beijing

Roots, Identity, and Living Heritage

Great Wall, imperial landmarks, and the continuity of Chinese civilisation. Hutong observation, calligraphy, and everyday cultural practice.

A module focused on how history, memory, and identity shape modern China

Shanghai

Global Interface, Exchange, and Modern Chinese Identity

The Bund, Pudong, and urban transformation. School exchange, peer dialogue, and neighbourhood discovery.

A module focused on global connection, cultural fusion, and city life in contemporary China

Shenzhen

Future Ecosystems, Innovation, and STEM Exploration

Technology ecosystems, robotics, and hands-on innovation. Smart mobility, urban change, and future city systems.

A module focused on how design, making, and innovation shape the world students are growing into

Hong Kong

Capstone Perspective, Sustainability, and Global Outlook

Harbour, density, and East–West urban histories. Field-based sustainability learning and community observation.

A module focused on comparison, reflection, and bringing the wider journey into sharper perspective

Additional city modules (Hangzhou, Chengdu, Xi'an) available on request.

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages is China Quest suitable for?
Designed for students aged 14–18, with content and supervision tailored to this age group.
How long are the programmes?
Core city modules are typically 5 days and can be combined into longer programmes.
Is China safe for student groups?
Yes. Programmes include bilingual leaders, 1:8 ratio, emergency procedures, and international insurance.
Can schools customise the itinerary?
Yes. Schools select modules, adjust length, and discuss themes. Each programme is co-designed.
Is the programme TY compatible?
Yes. Includes reflection tasks, portfolio outputs, and opportunities for collaboration and critical thinking.
Do students need to speak Chinese?
No. All leaders are bilingual and activities are delivered in English.
What is included in the fee?
Accommodation, meals, domestic transport, entry fees, activities, insurance, learning materials, and 24/7 support. Flights, visa fees, and personal spending not included.

Safety & Logistics

Caring for Our Students

Student safety and wellbeing is our absolute priority at every stage.

01

Professional Team & Teacher Involvement

Each group is accompanied by experienced bilingual leaders at a maximum 1:8 leader-to-student ratio. Schools are welcome to nominate a teacher to join, enhancing continuity and guided reflection.

02

Safety Protocols & Emergency Response

Comprehensive risk assessments before each programme. 24/7 bilingual support with defined emergency protocols including medical access, family communication, and travel adjustments — aligned with European school trip standards.

03

Insurance & Accommodation

All participants covered by international travel and health insurance. Quality 3–4 star hotels with supervised twin/triple room arrangements and all meals managed throughout the journey.

✓ What's Included

  • All accommodation (hotel, twin/triple)
  • All meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
  • All domestic transport in China
  • All entry fees & activity costs
  • International travel & health insurance
  • 24/7 bilingual support team

  • Certificate of completion

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about China Quest.

China Quest is designed for students aged 14–18. Our programme content, supervision ratios, and activities are tailored to this age group, ensuring age-appropriate challenge, safety, and engagement throughout.
We offer flexible modular programmes of 5, 10, or 14 days. Each city module is 5 days, and schools can combine modules to create longer itineraries. This modular design lets you tailor the experience to your school calendar and budget.
Yes. China has very low crime rates and is widely regarded as safe for travellers. Our programmes include comprehensive risk assessments, 24/7 bilingual support, a maximum 1:8 leader-to-student ratio, defined emergency protocols, and full international travel and health insurance. We've been operating on the ground in China for over 20 years.
The all-inclusive fee covers accommodation, all meals, domestic transport, entry fees, activities, insurance, learning materials, and 24/7 support. International flights, visa fees, and personal spending are not included. See our Safety & Logistics section for the full breakdown.
Absolutely. Our modular design means you can choose which cities to visit, how many days to spend, and even request specific themes or activities. We co-design each programme with the school to align with curriculum goals, student interests, and logistical requirements.
No — no Chinese language skills are required. All programme leaders are bilingual (English–Chinese), and activities are conducted in English. Students will pick up basic phrases naturally during the trip, which is part of the cultural immersion experience!
We have defined 24/7 emergency protocols including immediate medical access, direct family communication channels, and travel adjustment procedures. Each group has a dedicated emergency coordinator, and our protocols are aligned with European school trip standards.

Yes, China Quest is specifically designed to be TY-compatible. The programme includes structured reflection tasks, portfolio-building activities, and an optional certificate of completion — all aligned with Transition Year learning objectives around autonomy, collaboration, and critical thinking.

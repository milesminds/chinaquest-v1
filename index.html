China Quest is an immersive educational travel programme that brings students to some of China's most iconic cities. Blending real-world exploration, hands-on learning challenges, and guided reflection, the programme builds global awareness and personal insight in ways that no classroom can replicate.

Whether as a summer experience or a Transition Year (TY) international module, China Quest offers a powerful combination of travel, discovery, and growth — designed exclusively for leading international schools and private schools.

Experience China First-Hand

Travel through innovation hubs and historical cities for a ground-level understanding of modern China.

Explore Your Future Potential

Connect personal interests with China's evolving role in technology, education, and global society.

Challenge-Based Learning

Daily guided challenges in tech, arts, culture, and history nurture curiosity and critical thinking.

Build Real-World Confidence

Develop independence, adaptability, and resilience through authentic experiences and structured reflection.

Designed for TY Goals

Compatible with Transition Year frameworks — reflection tasks, optional certificate, portfolio creation.

Safe & Professionally Led

Expert-led with full pastoral care, logistics planning, and wellbeing management throughout.

Safety & Logistics

Caring for Our Students

Student safety and wellbeing is our absolute priority at every stage.

01

Professional Team & Teacher Involvement

Each group is accompanied by experienced bilingual leaders at a maximum 1:8 leader-to-student ratio. Schools are welcome to nominate a teacher to join, enhancing continuity and guided reflection.

02

Safety Protocols & Emergency Response

Comprehensive risk assessments before each programme. 24/7 bilingual support with defined emergency protocols including medical access, family communication, and travel adjustments — aligned with European school trip standards.

03

Insurance & Accommodation

All participants covered by international travel and health insurance. Quality 3–4 star hotels with supervised twin/triple room arrangements and all meals managed throughout the journey.

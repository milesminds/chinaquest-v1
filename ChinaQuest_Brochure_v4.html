Where Students Go

Four Core Modules Across Modern China

Each city module offers a distinct lens on China — from historical roots and cultural exchange to innovation, sustainability, and global perspective.

Core city modules are typically 5 days and can be combined into longer programmes tailored to your school.

Beijing

Roots, Identity, and Living Heritage

Great Wall, imperial landmarks, and the continuity of Chinese civilisation

Hutong observation, calligraphy, and everyday cultural practice

A module focused on how history, memory, and identity shape modern China

Shanghai

Global Interface, Exchange, and Modern Chinese Identity

The Bund, Pudong, and the story of urban transformation

School exchange, peer dialogue, and neighbourhood discovery

A module focused on global connection, cultural fusion, and city life in contemporary China

Shenzhen

Future Ecosystems, Innovation, and STEM Exploration

Technology ecosystems, robotics, and hands-on innovation exposure

Smart mobility, urban change, and future city systems

A module focused on how design, making, and innovation shape the world students are growing into

Hong Kong

Capstone Perspective, Sustainability, and Global Outlook

Harbour, density, and East–West urban histories

Field-based sustainability learning and local community observation

A module focused on comparison, reflection, and bringing the wider journey into sharper perspective

Additional city modules (Hangzhou, Chengdu, Xi'an) available on request.

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages is China Quest suitable for?
China Quest is designed for students aged 14–18, with content, supervision, and activities tailored to this age group.
How long are the programmes?
Programmes are built from city-based learning modules. Core modules are typically 5 days and can be combined into longer programmes.
Is China safe for student groups?
Yes. Programmes include experienced bilingual leaders, a 1:8 leader-to-student ratio, defined emergency procedures, and international travel and health insurance.
Can schools customise the itinerary?
Yes. Schools can select and combine modules, adjust programme length, and discuss specific themes or priorities.
Is the programme Transition Year compatible?
Yes. China Quest includes reflection tasks, portfolio-style outputs, and opportunities for collaboration, autonomy, and critical thinking.
Do students need to speak Chinese?
No. All programme leaders are bilingual and activities are delivered in English.
What is included in the programme fee?
Accommodation, meals, domestic transport, entry fees, activities, insurance, learning materials, and 24/7 support. International flights, visa fees, and personal spending are not included.

Programme Overview

What is China Quest?

China Quest helps students explore China through culture, technology, and real-world experience — with reflection, discussion, and meaningful outcomes built in.

Each day follows a structured learning cycle: Exploration → Reflection → Challenge → Circle. This daily rhythm ensures students do more than visit places — they learn through them.

City Modules

Four Core Modules Across Modern China

Each city module offers a distinct lens on China. Core modules are typically 5 days and can be combined into longer programmes tailored to your school.

Beijing

Roots, Identity, and Living Heritage

Great Wall, imperial landmarks, and the continuity of Chinese civilisation. Hutong observation, calligraphy, and everyday cultural practice.

A module focused on how history, memory, and identity shape modern China.

Shanghai

Global Interface, Exchange, and Modern Chinese Identity

The Bund, Pudong, and the story of urban transformation. School exchange, peer dialogue, and neighbourhood discovery.

A module focused on global connection, cultural fusion, and city life in contemporary China.

Shenzhen

Future Ecosystems, Innovation, and STEM Exploration

Technology ecosystems, robotics, and hands-on innovation exposure. Smart mobility, urban change, and future city systems.

A module focused on how design, making, and innovation shape the world students are growing into.

Hong Kong

Capstone Perspective, Sustainability, and Global Outlook

Harbour, density, and East–West urban histories. Field-based sustainability learning and local community observation.

A module focused on comparison, reflection, and bringing the wider journey into sharper perspective.

Additional city modules (Hangzhou, Chengdu, Xi'an) are available on request for schools seeking a broader or more tailored programme.

Student Outcomes

What Students Gain

Students return with more than memories — they gain confidence, perspective, and evidence of meaningful learning.

Tangible Learning Evidence

Students return with documented observations, reflective journals, creative outputs, and a certificate of completion that can support TY portfolio work. Depending on the module, students may also produce interview notes, short research tasks, visual documentation, or digital storytelling work.

Global Fluency & Core Competencies

Through teamwork, reflection, and cross-cultural engagement, students strengthen adaptability, communication, collaboration, and critical thinking. The programme helps students move beyond passive observation and develop the confidence to interpret, question, and respond to unfamiliar environments.

Career & Future Pathways

Exposure to innovation, urban change, and professional environments helps students connect academic interests with future possibilities. From technology ecosystems to cultural institutions and university settings, students gain first-hand insight into the worlds they may one day study in, work in, or help shape.

Safety & School Support

Supporting Student Wellbeing

China Quest is designed to support student wellbeing, clear supervision, and practical collaboration with schools throughout the journey.

Clear Supervision

Students are supported by experienced bilingual leaders, with clear group supervision and structured daily coordination throughout the programme.

Risk-Aware Planning

Programmes are delivered with risk awareness built in — including route planning, activity review, emergency response procedures, and clear communication protocols.

School-Facing Support

Schools are welcome to nominate a teacher to accompany the group, helping strengthen continuity, pastoral support, and alignment with school expectations.

Student Wellbeing

Accommodation, transport, meals, and day-to-day logistics are planned to support student comfort and safety. Insurance cover, access to medical support, and clear communication procedures are built into programme planning.
